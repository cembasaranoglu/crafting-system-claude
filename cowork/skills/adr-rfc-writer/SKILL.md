# adr-rfc-writer

Produce ADR or RFC artifacts that cite real source paths, separate fact from assumption, and refuse to claim validation that was not run. Use the templates in `prompt-system/templates/adr/` and `prompt-system/templates/rfc/`. Bound the change set, list trade offs, and call out blockers.
